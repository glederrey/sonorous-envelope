## Feedback for Claude Code

1. **Desktop scrollbar arrows.** In Brave on desktop, the panel scrollbar is thin with no grey track, but the arrows are still there. Expected: no arrows (per "What changed"). Gael also asks for the phone behaviour on desktop: only the bar, hidden once scrolling stops (an overlay scrollbar). He prefers how it looks on the phone.
2. **A covered window counts as visible.** With the Brave tab active but its window covered by the Claude desktop app, the player kept taking pictures (`age_s: 28` after about 90 s). Minimizing triggered the "only while visible" note as expected. This is probably the Page Visibility API treating a covered window as visible. Either say in the brief and the `of` text that the player must be minimized or in a background tab, or also use window blur/focus if a covered window should count as hidden.
3. **Spectrogram shorter after coming back.** The first picture after returning had `measured.seconds: 7.6`, although Strudel had played the whole time. The brief allows "less right after the switch", but this was a return from hidden, not a switch. If the audio buffer pauses while hidden, that is fine; otherwise expected about 10 s.
4. **Outside the brief: repeated turn number.** The `set_sound` that switches mode and the next call share the same `turn`: 5 and 5 (switch to Attuned, then the first Attuned call), 10 and 10 (switch to Jukebox, then `strudel_mode` on). Minor, but `inspect` history may merge them.
5. **Outside the brief: joy cannot be corrected under a frozen dorian voice.** In D dorian, joy 0.7 read 0.39 (`reads_why: held down by mode, smoothness`): a gap of 0.31, over the correction threshold. The correction with joy 0.85 still read 0.41. The rules ask for one correction, but nothing short of a new voice can close the gap. Maybe say so in the result ("change the voice's scale to raise joy").
6. **Outside the brief: roughness on calm sounds (still open from v0.22.0, item 12).** `sound.roughness` read 0.85–0.96 for the calm, consonant D dorian state (triangle, drone, 1/4 rate, no tension), and 0.33–0.57 for the livelier states.
7. **Tester error, not a product bug.** Once, the tester sent `listen` in the same batch as the final `set_sound`, against the rules. Gael stopped the call. No product fault was seen.
