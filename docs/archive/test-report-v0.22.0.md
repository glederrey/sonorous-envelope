## Feedback for Claude Code

1. **`listen` misses a press claimed by a concurrent call.** What happened: with the final `set_sound` and `listen` in one parallel batch, Gael pressed Calmer after the listening line appeared; `set_sound` returned it (`"calmer, 1 s ago"`) and `listen` waited 20 s and returned `{"event":"idle","waited_s":20}`, so the press got no answer in that message. Expected: `listen` returns any press that lands after it starts, even one another call has marked read, or the Attuned/Jukebox guidance and the `listen` description say plainly that it must be called after the final call has returned, never in the same batch.
2. **Listening line not shown on one wait.** On try 2 of test 6 (sequential calls, turn 10), `listen` waited 20 s, but Gael saw only "Live tuning is on: the AI listens after its next answer", never "The AI is listening … (N s)". On the next try it showed correctly. Worth checking whether the player can miss the start of a wait.
3. **A line while the model answers a press.** After a press, the panel goes back to "Live tuning is on: the AI listens after its next answer" while the model is still working on that press, then listens again. Gael suggests an in-between line, e.g. "The AI is answering your press…".
4. **What "Live tuning" means after an idle wait.** Gael proposes that live tuning turn itself off after 20 s with no press. My view: keep the toggle as the listener's standing wish, but after `idle` show "Not listening now: your presses reach the AI with your next message"; or turn it off only after several idle waits in a row (e.g. 3). Decide between these.
5. **"More of this" / "Less of this" are ambiguous.** I read "More of this" as "keep it" and resent the same code ("same code and tempo as before: the music does not change"), so Gael heard nothing happen and asked whether the buttons should go. Options: rename them "Keep this" / "Not this", or define them in the guidance as "go further in the last direction" / "undo the last change", so a press always gives an audible answer.
6. **"Reproach" is unclear.** Gael didn't know what it meant. Add a tooltip, or use a plainer word such as "scolding" or "blaming".
7. **Motif notes outside the voice.** The test motif (E4 G4 A4 C5 B4) over an F# lydian voice sounded "not in a complete tune" to Gael. Nothing in the result says the notes are outside the voice's scale. Suggest a note in the result (e.g. "C5 is outside F# lydian") or an option to snap to the voice. Also, Gael heard the three-note quote as "lower in pitch" than the original, although the notes were identical (probably because the climb to C5 is missing). Just an observation.
8. **Snapshot ≠ what the listener sees with Strudel.** The snapshot shows a bright pianoroll on light green, without the code overlay and without the dimming Gael sees. Gael would like the AI to see exactly what he sees. Suggest capturing the composite as displayed, including the code when "Strudel code" is on, so problems like hard-to-read code or a too-dark screen are visible to the model.
9. **Code before music on reload.** After a reload with Strudel on, the code appears before the music and drawings, which wait for the next bar. Gael asks that the code fade in on the same bar.
10. **Panel scrollbar look.** Works, but Gael finds it not very nice-looking (he has already sent visual feedback).
11. **Brief wording for test 7.** A press comes back twice: in `listen` and again in the next sound call, which marks it read. That is by design, but "shows up exactly once in a result" in the brief reads as if a second appearance were a failure. Suggest "is marked read exactly once".
12. **Outside the brief: roughness on calm sounds.** `sound.roughness` read 0.73 for calm, consonant Attuned states with no tension (turns 6 and 17: F# lydian, 1/4 rate, drone on), and 0.65 for the aurora state, versus ~0.3 for the livelier ones. Maybe the drone plus a sparse arp counts as dissonant. Worth a look if roughness is meant to track dissonance.
13. **Outside the brief: cold mood reads warm.** Warmth 0.3 (cold, snow) gave a sawtooth voice but read warmth 0.56, `reads_why: "lifted by smoothness, soft_timbre"`: a gap of 0.26, just under the correction threshold.
14. **Test 17 done in Claude Code** (row 17). Side observation from that session: Self in D dorian read `joy` ≈ 0.4 when 0.6–0.65 was sent (`reads_why: held down by mode`), under the correction threshold.
